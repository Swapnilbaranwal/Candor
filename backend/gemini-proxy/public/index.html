Candor

Explore India. Pay what locals pay.

An honest travel companion for visitors to India. Candor plans your days, tells you the fair price for any ride or purchase before you pay, and answers anything in your language.

The problem

  • Visitors can't tell a fair price from an inflated one. Autos run without meters, menus have no prices, and the same item costs different amounts in different places.
  • A few well-known tricks, like "the monument is closed" or "your hotel burned down", give a whole city a bad name, even though most sellers are honest.
  • Language makes it harder. Visitors can't ask, bargain or get help in Hindi, so they either overpay or stop trusting everyone.

Why the solution holds up

  • Grounded, not guessed. Gemini can't judge a price without first calling a tool: the Delhi Transport Dept. fare chart, the printed MRP, official ASI ticket prices or what other travellers paid.
  • Every step is shown. The traveller sees which tools ran and the source of the fair range, so they can check the answer.
  • A range, not a promise. Candor gives a fair range with room for traffic and rounding, and says when it isn't sure.
  • Polite, not suspicious. Each verdict comes with a respectful line to say in Hindi, so a price talk stays friendly.
  • Gets better with use. Travellers can share what they paid, anonymously, so prices reflect what people pay today.

AI and Google tools

  • A Gemini agent checks prices with tools. Gemini also reads photos of price tags, writes the guide for each place, plans days from your interests and answers questions.
  • Keys stay in Secret Manager on Cloud Run. Neither the app nor this page ever holds them.

Social impact

  • Trust in both directions. Visitors pay a fair price, and honest drivers and shopkeepers stop being lumped in with the few who overcharge.
  • Data a city can act on. Anonymous scam reports are grouped by area, and Gemini turns them into a brief for the tourism office or tourist police.
  • Income for local guides. Verified guides with fixed, visible rates get bookings without middlemen.
  • Safety and access. SOS with 112, a way back to your hotel from anywhere, and help in your own language.
  • Better tourism for India. Travellers who feel treated fairly stay longer, spend more locally and come back.

This is the web version of the Candor iPhone app, running on Google Cloud Run. Prices are guidance, not a guarantee. In an emergency in India, call 112.
